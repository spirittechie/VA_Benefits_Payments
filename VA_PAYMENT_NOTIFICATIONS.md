# VA Benefits Payment Text Notifications

Veterans receiving recurring disability compensation or pension payments can opt in to payment text notifications directly through VA.gov. No third-party registration is required.

## Enable VA payment notifications

1. Sign in to [VA.gov](https://www.va.gov/).
2. Open your name in the menu and select [Profile](https://www.va.gov/profile/).
3. Under Notification settings, select **Manage notification settings**.
4. Under Payments, find **Disability and pension deposit notifications**.
5. Select **Notify me by text**.

Enrollment on or before the 10th of the month applies to that month's payment; enrollment after the 10th begins with the following month's payment.

## Observed monthly notification dates

The project maintainer recorded the following payment-processing text notifications in 2026:

| Month | Date received | Day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| July | July 22, 2026 | Wednesday |
| August | August 22, 2026 | Saturday |
| September | September 22, 2026 | Tuesday |
| October | October 22, 2026 | Thursday |
| November | November 22, 2026 | Sunday |

All five observations occurred on the 22nd, including weekends. This suggests a possible fixed calendar-day schedule rather than a banking-day schedule. These observations are from one recipient and do not establish an official or nationwide VA notification schedule. The 22nd should be treated as an observed pattern, not a guaranteed date.

## What a payment-processing notification means

A text stating that a recurring payment is being processed does not establish that an ACH transfer has completed, that the receiving institution has received the funds, or that the funds are available for withdrawal.

The project's estimated ACH notice calendar is a separate planning aid, not an official VA notification service or a confirmed ACH transmission schedule. Subscribe to the VA text service for direct notifications.

## Troubleshoot missing notifications

- **Timing:** If you enrolled after the 10th, the first notification is for the following month's payment.
- **Previous opt-out:** If you previously texted STOP, send **START to 96702** from the same mobile number associated with your VA.gov profile. Profile settings alone may not restore messages. See the [VA's instructions for restoring text notifications](https://www.va.gov/resources/how-to-start-getting-va-text-messages-again-after-youve-opted-out/).
- **Settings:** Confirm your mobile number and that **Notify me by text** is selected under disability and pension deposit notifications in your VA.gov profile.
- **Missing payment:** Review your [VA payment history](https://www.va.gov/va-payment-history/).

If the payment is missing or texts still do not arrive after restoring notifications, contact VA at **800-827-1000**.

## Security and privacy

VA will not ask for login credentials or fees by text. Verify suspicious messages directly through VA.gov or by calling VA.

## Community verification

Veterans are invited to report the date they received their payment-processing notification through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/spirittechie/VA_Benefits_Payments/issues). Please provide only the date and whether the message stated that payment was being processed. Do not include compensation amounts, phone numbers, account information, unredacted screenshots, or other personal information.

Additional observations can help establish whether the 22nd is consistent across recipients and identify exceptions.
